My hw3 gives each enemy an HP of 10, the player an HP of 20, the base an HP of 50, and each bullet 5 damage points.
As the player approaches the enemies (detection angle 60 degrees), the enemies turn toward the player and shoot.
As the player runs away, the enemies go back to shooting the base.
When the player kills all enemies, the win scene is called; When either the player or the base lose all HP, the lose scene is called.
